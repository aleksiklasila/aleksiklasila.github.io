Leveling & stacks
  • Most buildings/items scale with stacks. Displayed level is based on stacks (roughly $L=\lfloor\log_2(\text{stacks})\rfloor+1$).
  • You may see base vs effective values in the right panel; effective values include adjacency/group/area bonuses.
  • Units also show their own level (for example `L1`, `L2`) and subgroup toggles are level-aware.
Areas & adjacency
  • Connected structures/items form groups; bigger connected groups can increase effective power.
  • Each tile belongs to an area. Area-level multipliers (M0..M5) increase output for objects inside that area.
  • Area Up upgrades are available when an area is fully filled and can be leveled up step by step.
Economy basics
  • Collectors gather from gold mines; farms provide passive income over time.
  • Barracks/spawners queue units at purchase-time costs and use their current rally target on spawn.
Core status effects
  • Fire: burst damage + burn over time.
  • Poison: poison damage over time.
  • Ice: slow/freeze pressure.
  • Water: wet/soak state, often enabling stronger ice follow-up.
  • Sand: movement slow/control.
  • Watch: marks targets for vision sharing (revealed while watched).
Combinations & counters
  • Water + Ice is a key combo path (wet targets are easier to lock down).
  • Layering DoTs (Fire/Poison) with control (Ice/Sand) is effective against groups.
  • Some unit lines are resistant/immune to specific elements or tower damage types.
  • Some tower families resist their own effect type; mix damage types to break through.
Win conditions
  • Destroy Base: eliminate enemy core/base structures.
  • Kill King: defeat the enemy king unit to win.
Useful strategic notes
  • Use rally splitting to spread reinforcements or stage flanks.
  • Use minimap right-click to set distant rallies/commands instantly.
  • Auto-upgrade + salvage controls in the right panel help macro-management.
