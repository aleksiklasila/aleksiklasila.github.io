# UI polish and spike hunt — 2026-10-17

Rule from the user: every spike found gets a cause and a fix, measured before
and after in real Edge on the 100k fixture (`tests/100000-1000.json`).

## Tools added

- `tests/ui-fuzz.cjs` — random real input (headed Edge, solo match): clicks,
  click chains into menus/submenus, drags, wheel, keys, with and without
  Ctrl/Shift. Checks after every step: page errors, native drags not blocked,
  stray page selections, hover text visible without a modifier, drags still
  active with every button up, scrolled layout boxes, canvas off its area,
  menus off screen, frames over 250 ms, TPS. `QUERY=guards=0` turns the page
  guards off (to check the fuzzer still catches what they prevent).
- `tests/action-spike.cjs <fixture> <action>...` — one action at a time
  (`click:` / `select:` / `key:` / `eval:`, `xN` repeats): longest frame and
  long task, ticks, and a CPU profile of the busiest stretch (total + self per
  function). `VIEW=3d`, `SHOT=`, `TRACE=1`.
- `tests/view-switch-spike.cjs` — the 2D/3D switch (first and later ones),
  `PROFILE=1`, `TRACE=`.
- `tests/edge-cdp.cjs` now also returns `send` (raw CDP, e.g. real mouse input).

## Still open (cause known)

1. ~~Per-tick unit column upload~~: fixed (table above). Measured after
   (headed Edge, solo): 100k `action-spike eval:0x8` VIEW=3d 0 frames over
   50 ms; render bench FPS 100-102, TPS 20, max frame ≤ 37 ms; 200k Full and
   Team FPS 96-101, TPS 20, max ≤ 40 ms; multiplayer 100k host + guest FPS
   95-100, TPS 19.5-20. (FPS ~100 is this display setup's ceiling today: an
   empty page runs 98-102.) Dead end: copying px/py from the last frame's
   x/y on the GPU (`copyBufferSubData` in the same buffer) took Edge from
   ~100 to ~37 FPS (ANGLE copies through its staging buffer and waits). At
   600k units the simulation is the limit (TPS 17-18.6 with or without the
   presentation helpers, interleaved A/B); frames come every 4th tick there.
7. ~~Team + history at 200k/team: page FPS 50-60, ~35 frames over 50 ms in
   10 s~~: fixed (table above). The rarer frames over 50 ms in all modes:
   the worst (117-204 ms) were V8's memory-reducer GC, fixed (table above).
   **Still open** (2026-10-17 evening, 200k solo, headed Edge, 30 s cases):
   one or two 58-90 ms frames in about half the cases at ~100 Hz (after:
   9 in 18 cases, max 86 ms; before: 6 in 9 combat cases, max 204 ms), and
   more once the window was on the 120 Hz display (26 in 18, max 208 ms).
   Three causes, traced: (a) the memory reducer still runs and now pauses
   54-58 ms (V8's own compaction, page release and a safepoint that waits
   for a background compile); it runs when V8 sees a low allocation rate,
   so less garbage can mean more of them; (b) GC helpers and the GPU main
   thread lose the CPU: a 31 ms scavenge had its helper off-CPU 29 ms
   (trace `tdur`); the renderer runs the simulation worker + 7 helpers and
   the presentation worker + 3 helpers on 16 logical CPUs (~8.4 cores busy
   in combat); "JS spikes" such as `updateBottomBar` 52 ms or
   `updateControlGroupBar` 48 ms were scavenges stalled inside them;
   (c) at 120 Hz, combat 3d-far needs 6.1 ms of GPU a frame (recent worst
   15 ms, renderer3d `gpuFrameMs`): the GPU queue fills and a WebGL flush
   waited 65 ms in the GPU process (0.7 ms of CPU). Not GC there.
   Probes for this (`tests/render-tps-bench.cjs`): `LONG_MS` (each long
   frame with the page's work by part and its long-animation-frame
   scripts: a page-idle frame is GC or the browser, LoAF does not count
   GC), `TRACE_VIEW`/`TRACE_SAVE`/`TRACE_CATS` (every major GC with its
   parts and reason), `ALLOC=1`, `HEAPPROF_VIEW`, `gpuMs` in rows; heap
   census `.claude/heapcensus.cjs`, `.claude/tracegaps.cjs`,
   `.claude/tracecpu.cjs`. Traced and heap-profiled cases count extra long
   frames at their end (the probe's own stop): judge counts untraced.
   Render bench rows count frames over 33 and 50 ms
   (`frameMs.over33/over50`). Multiplayer 100k (host + guest, one machine,
   12-35% busy with other programs): Team + history 59-76 FPS, TPS 12.5-20;
   Team in the same run 55-68 FPS, TPS 10-18 (both below the earlier 95-100
   / 20: the machine's load, both modes alike).
