HOW TO PLAY

  • Defend the castle at the center with turrets & items.
  • STACK: Determines level. Level improves damage, range, cooldown.
  • UPGRADE: Stack duplicate cards on existing towers to upgrade.
  • MONEY: Unbought cards give 50% cash at wave end.
  • LOCK: Long press a card for 500ms to lock (prevents auto-sell).
  • SALVAGE: Long click (>0.5s) a tower/item to mark for salvage.
  • GROUPS: Connect similar towers to multiply effective stacks by group size.
  • AREAS: Fill outlined areas with one group to multiply stacks by area size.
  • ELEMENTS: Fire, Poison, Ice, Water.
  • SYNERGY: Active effects combine their damage.
  • WATER: Deals 0 damage alone. Needs other effects to trigger damage & synergy.
